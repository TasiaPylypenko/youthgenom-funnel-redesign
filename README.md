# YouthGenom funnel redesign

Product Designer home task: conversion rate optimisation of the YouthGenom quiz funnel (social ad → quiz → plan → payment) for women 50+ in peri- and postmenopause, 98% on mobile.

## Try the prototype

**[Open the clickable prototype →](https://tasiapylypenko.github.io/youthgenom-funnel-redesign/prototype/us-en/g1-22m-usd/0-landing/)**

- Made for phones: open it on your phone, or in your browser's mobile view (375 px wide).
- [All screens](https://tasiapylypenko.github.io/youthgenom-funnel-redesign/prototype/) lets you jump to any screen.
- Your answers stay in your own browser. Opening the landing starts a clean run.
- The redesign ends at the paywall; checkout isn't part of it.

## What's here

| | |
|---|---|
| [Analysis](docs/analysis.md) | The data, where women leave and why, the three problems that cost the most, the redesign and the expected impact |

| [Prototype source](prototype/) | The clickable prototype: plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no build step |

The prototype is the source of truth for behaviour; Figma is the source of truth for layout and components. Where they could differ, the spec says which one wins.
